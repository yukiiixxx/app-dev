# 🖤 My Favorite Series: *Wednesday*

---

## 📖 Overview
*Wednesday* is a **dark comedy and mystery series** created by **Alfred Gough** and **Miles Millar**, and directed by **Tim Burton**.  
It follows **Wednesday Addams**, a witty and emotionless teenager obsessed with the macabre.  
The show premiered on **Netflix** and became a hit for its gothic visuals, sharp humor, and intriguing supernatural mystery.

---

## 👩‍🎓 Main Characters
- **Wednesday Addams** — A gothic teenager with psychic powers  
- **Enid Sinclair** — Her colorful and cheerful roommate  
- **Xavier Thorpe** — A mysterious student and artist  
- **Principal Larissa Weems** — The head of Nevermore Academy  
- **Thing** — A loyal disembodied hand who assists Wednesday  

---

## 🔮 Plot Summary
> “I act as if I don’t care if people like me. Deep down… I secretly enjoy it.” — *Wednesday Addams*

After a *“piranha incident”* at her previous school, Wednesday is sent to **Nevermore Academy**, a place for outcasts.  
There, she discovers her psychic powers and becomes entangled in a murder mystery linked to her family’s dark past.  
As she investigates, Wednesday learns more about herself — and what it means to be different.

---

## 💀 Why I Love It
1. Gothic and dark aesthetic 🕸️  
2. Clever mystery with unexpected twists  
3. Wednesday’s sarcastic humor and confidence  
4. Inspiring message about individuality and friendship
